## 最近输入

> 用 Obsidian 的 Dataview 插件启用后，可以自动列出最近笔记：
> ```dataview
> TABLE file.ctime AS 时间, type AS 类型
> FROM "00-Inbox" OR "01-Sources"
> SORT file.ctime DESC
> LIMIT 10
> ```
