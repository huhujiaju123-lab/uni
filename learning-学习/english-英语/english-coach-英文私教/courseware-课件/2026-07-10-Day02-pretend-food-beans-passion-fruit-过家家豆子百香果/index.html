Word Expansion｜词汇深挖

Deep Word

texture

How something feels in your mouth or under your fingers.

用嘴巴或手感受到的感觉：口感、触感、材质。

Food

soft texture
crunchy texture
juicy texture

Fabric

soft blanket
rough towel
smooth silk

Fruit

juicy pulp
tiny seeds
sour taste

Parenting

What does it feel like?
Is it soft or hard?

Mini Practice｜即时练习

填空：We are playing ______ food.

Answer: pretend

pretend food = 假装食物 / 过家家食物。

选择：豆荚用哪个词？

Answer: pod

bean pod 是豆荚。

改错：The passion fruit has many seed.

Answer: The passion fruit has many seeds.

可数名词复数要加 s。

表达：这个摸起来很硬。

Answer: It feels hard.

feel + adjective 描述触感。

表达：它有点酸。

Answer: It tastes a little sour.

taste + adjective 描述味道。

Speak It Yourself｜开口挑战

请用英文描述你和孩子观察豆子、打开豆荚、尝百香果的过程。

Keywords: pretend food, beans, pod, passion fruit, seeds, pulp, texture, sour

查看参考答案

My daughter and I are playing pretend food. We look at different beans together. Some beans are small, and some are bigger. We open the pod and see what is inside. Then we talk about passion fruit. It has seeds and pulp. It has a soft, juicy texture, and it tastes a little sour.
