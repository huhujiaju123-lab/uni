Key Expressions｜核心表达

Word Expansion｜词汇深挖

Deep Word

anchor

Something that keeps another thing steady, fixed, or grounded.

让某物稳定、固定、扎住的东西。核心不是“锚”，而是 steady。

Boat

drop anchor
The boat is steady.

Life

My family is my anchor.
This habit anchors my day.

Learning

A clear image anchors a word.
Concept anchors memory.

News

news anchor
a person who holds the show together

Mini Practice｜即时练习

填空：A morning routine can ______ your day.

Answer: anchor

anchor 在这里是动词：稳定。

选择：anchor 的核心画面是什么？

Answer: something that keeps things steady

不要先想中文“锚”。

改错：I want know Chinese meaning.

Answer: I want to know the Chinese meaning.

want to do something。

表达：我想深入理解一个词。

Answer: I want to understand one word deeply.

这是 Day03 的核心句。

表达：这个习惯稳定了我的一天。

Answer: This habit anchors my day.

anchor your day 是自然搭配。
