Today’s Story｜今日情境

🛣️

I drive up the ramp and follow the signs to the exit.

从坡道开上去，跟着出口标识走。

🚧

The gate goes up.

闸门升起来。

🚗

I pull onto the road and come to a red light.

驶入道路，遇到红灯。

🚦

The light turns green, so I signal right and change lanes.

绿灯后打右转灯并变道。

🌿

The leaves perked up after I watered the plant.

浇水后叶子精神起来。

🌳

Both sides of the road were lined with trees.

道路两旁排列着树。

Fixes｜易错点

Plural

both sides of the road
leaves

Uncountable

The stem carries water from the soil to the leaves.

Chunk

Do not memorize pull alone. Memorize pull onto the road.

Mini Practice｜即时练习

改错：I need to change a lane.

Answer: I need to change lanes.

填空：The stem carries ______ from the soil to the leaves.

Answer: water

表达：闸门升起来。

Answer: The gate goes up.

表达：道路两旁都是树。

Answer: Both sides of the road were lined with trees.

表达：叶子浇水后精神起来。

Answer: The leaves perked up after I watered the plant.

Speak It Yourself｜开口挑战

用英文说 45 秒：描述你从停车场开出去、进入道路、看到路边植物的过程。

Keywords: drive up the ramp, follow the signs, gate goes up, pull onto the road, red light, change lanes, lined with trees, leaves perk up

查看参考答案

I drive up the ramp and follow the signs to the exit. When I get to the gate, the gate goes up. Then I pull onto the road and come to a red light. I wait at the red light, and when the light turns green, I signal right and change lanes. Both sides of the road are lined with trees. Some branches sway in the wind, and the leaves look fresh, like they perked up after the rain.
