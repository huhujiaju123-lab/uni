Driving to Work on a Rainy Day

雨天开车去上班：从真实生活出发，把“我想表达的话”升级成自然、地道、可反复使用的英语。

rain on and offon my waypull awaywindshield wiperspedal

Today’s Story｜今日情境

真实生活里的语言，比教材对话更容易记住。

🚗

On my way to the office

正在开车去公司。

🌧️

It’s raining on and off today.

今天的雨断断续续地下。

🔴

The light turned red, so I stopped.

红灯了，所以我停了下来。

↔️

It’s green for the other direction.

对向是绿灯，所以那些车在通行。

🟢

I pressed the accelerator and pulled away.

我踩下油门，起步驶离。

What I Said｜我的原始表达

“My English is so so.”

想表达“英语还不太好”。更自然的说法会更积极。

“It’s rain heavily.”

意思清楚，但这里需要正在进行时：It’s raining.

“I’m driving in the car.”

日常场景更常说：I’m on my way to...

“The other direction goes.”

需要把“哪个方向是绿灯、车在移动”说完整。

Natural English Upgrade｜地道表达升级

What I said

My English is so so.

Correct

My English isn’t very good yet.

Natural

I’m still improving my English.

What I said

It’s rain heavily.

Correct

It’s raining heavily.

Natural

It’s raining on and off today. Sometimes it comes down heavily.

What I said

I move.

Correct

I started moving.

Natural

I pressed the accelerator and pulled away.

Word Expansion｜词汇深挖

Core Word

pedal

A part that you press or move with your foot.

任何用脚踩、用脚操作的装置。不只是自行车踏板。

🚲 Bicycle

keep pedaling

🚗 Car

clutch pedal

🎹 Piano

sustain pedal

🎸 Guitar

effects pedal

🧵 Sewing Machine

foot pedal

Common Collocations

press the pedal

step on the brake

hit the brakes

English Thinking

不要只记：pedal = 踏板

要记：pedal = anything you press with your foot

Pronunciation Lab｜发音训练

steering wheel

STEER-ing wheel

不是 steer wheel。中间需要 -ing。

wipers

/ˈwaɪpərz/

开头是 /waɪ/，不是 weepers。结尾是浊辅音 /z/。

pull away

pull uh-WAY

重音在 away。表示车子起步驶离。

Grammar in Context｜情境语法

It rains.

一般事实或习惯。

It is raining.

现在正在下雨。

It has been raining.

雨已经下了一段时间。

The light turned red, so I stopped.

用过去时讲刚刚发生的开车场景。turned red 和 stopped 都是过去动作。

Mini Practice｜即时练习

选择题 1：今天雨时下时停，怎么说更自然？

Answer: It’s raining on and off today.

选择题 2：“我在去公司的路上”怎么说？

Answer: I’m on my way to the office.

填空题 1：I turned on the windshield ______.

Answer: wipers

填空题 2：The light turned red, so I ______.

Answer: stopped

改错题 1：It’s rain heavily.

Answer: It’s raining heavily.

改错题 2：Steer wheel

Answer: steering wheel

场景表达：绿灯后你踩油门起步。

Reference: I pressed the accelerator and pulled away.

Speak It Yourself｜开口挑战

请用英文描述：你正在开车去上班；今天的雨时下时停；你在红灯前停车；绿灯后踩下油门起步；你打开了雨刷。

Keywords: on my way, raining on and off, turned red, stopped, accelerator, pulled away, windshield wipers

查看参考答案

I’m on my way to the office. It’s raining on and off today, and sometimes it comes down heavily. The light turned red, so I stopped. When the light turned green, I pressed the accelerator and pulled away. I also turned on the windshield wipers.
