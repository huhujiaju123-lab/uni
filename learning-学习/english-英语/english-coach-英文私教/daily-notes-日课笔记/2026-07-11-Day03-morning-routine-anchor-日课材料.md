## Original → Natural English 表达升级

| My Original English | More Natural English | 中文说明 |
|---|---|---|
| I just woke up and I think I had a good sleep last night. | I just woke up. I think I slept well last night. | slept well 更自然。 |
| But I didn't completely woke up for now. | I don't feel fully awake yet. | 直接描述状态，不硬套 wake up。 |
| 我没有力气 | I don't have much energy today. | 最自然、最通用。 |
| I'm only 70% charged. | I feel like I'm only about 70% charged today. | 加 feel like 和 about 更自然。 |
| We didn't have enough understanding. | I don't think I fully understand this word yet. | 用 I don't think... 更自然。 |
| Use this word flexible. | Use this word flexibly. / Use this word naturally. | flexible 是形容词，flexibly 是副词。 |

## Today's Star Word｜anchor

### Core Image 核心意象

> A ship stays steady because the anchor holds it.

不要先记：

> anchor = 锚

要先记：

> anchor = something that keeps you steady.

### Meanings 词义网络

| Meaning Type | Core Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Physical noun | 让船不漂走的重物 | The anchor keeps the ship steady. |
| Verb | 抛锚、停泊 | The boat anchored near the island. |
| Psychological noun | 让人稳定的人或事 | My daughter is my anchor. |
| Figurative verb | 让某人或某天稳定下来 | Morning exercise anchors my day. |
| Professional noun | 主持人、主播 | She is a news anchor. |

## Biggest Takeaway 今日最大收获

今天真正学习的不是 `anchor`，而是：

> How to learn vocabulary.

不要只问：

> What's the Chinese translation?

要开始问：

> What's the picture?

当你记住核心意象，词就不再只是要背的东西，而是可以真正使用的东西。
