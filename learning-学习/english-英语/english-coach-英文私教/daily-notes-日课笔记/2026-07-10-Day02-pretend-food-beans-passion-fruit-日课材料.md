## Deep Word｜texture

### Core Meaning 核心概念

> Texture is how something feels in your mouth or under your fingers.

texture 不是简单的“质地”，而是你用嘴巴或手感受到的感觉。

### Different Scenes 不同场景

| Scene | Example |
|---|---|
| Food | The passion fruit has a juicy texture. |
| Fabric | This blanket has a soft texture. |
| Skin | Her skin feels smooth. |
| Beans | The beans feel hard and dry. |

### Common Collocations 高频搭配

| Collocation | 中文 |
|---|---|
| a soft texture | 柔软的质地 |
| a crunchy texture | 脆脆的口感 |
| a smooth texture | 顺滑的口感 / 触感 |
| a rough texture | 粗糙的触感 |
| a juicy texture | 多汁的口感 |

## One-Minute Reuse 1 分钟复用

用今天的表达，说一段和孩子玩过家家的英文：

```text
My daughter and I are playing pretend food.
She is pretending to cook.
We look at different beans together.
Some beans are small, and some are bigger.
We open the pod and see what's inside.
Then we talk about passion fruit.
It has a soft, juicy texture, and it tastes a little sour.
```
